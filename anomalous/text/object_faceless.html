The 12th Note

These Notes Appear In Random Places At Random Times And Are Of Unknown Origin

The 1st note reads;

Wherever the number of trees
Is greater than three
It will appear
It tolls for thee
You may try to hide
You may try to flee
But it will still find you
And you will be
Gone

The 2nd note reads;

The entity seems to exclusively take an interest in human beings. Visually, the entity's appearance vaguely resembles a human body. Biologically, the entity is very different from human beings. It is bipedal and hairless, and it has a similar range of motion to that of human bodies. It has no facial features, no orifices, no texture on the skin. Its entire body is like a stone smoothed by the ocean waves.

The 3rd note reads;

It always appears in dark places full of shadows where it is hard to see. A light source may still illuminate parts of its body, such as the face, the torso or the limbs. It will never allow any light source to illuminate its entire body at once. Everyone who encounters the entity disappears and is never heard from again. In photographs, it almost always appears as a white smudge that may sometimes resemble a humanoid shape.

The 4th note reads;

Those who do know of the entity only learn of its existence through the notes that describe it. These notes appear in random places at random times and are of unknown origin. Everyone seems to struggle with remembering that the entity exists when not directly looking at it, a photo of it, or one of the notes connected to it.

The 5th note reads;

Scientists have been unsuccessful in containing and studying the entity. They have also all forgotten it. They cannot protect you from it, no matter how much they try.

The 6th note reads;

These notes are an invitation.

The 7th note reads;

These notes are a warning.

The 8th note reads;

It spreads through being known. It is too late. The first was more than enough. Most people have disappeared by now. You are so, so lucky. Its persistent silence is suffocating. Obedience was choked out of me. I am frozen, and always will be. Even as I am aware of what I am doing to you, I cannot stop it, I am deeply sorry, I despise this fate.

The 9th note reads;

WARNING! DANGER! THESE NOTES CONTAIN A FATE WORSE THAN DEATH. STOP READING AND BURN THEM. BURN THIS NOTE. BURN IT. BURN.

The 10th note reads;

Scientists have been successful in containing the entity. The notes connected to its activity, however, are still known to appear occasionally, though it appears they have been rendered harmless. People still forget that the entity is real when not looking directly at it and staff in charge of containing it must regularly remind each other of its existence in order to continue successful containment. It is vital that the containment procedure is recorded and preserved. The containment procedure is [REDACTED].

The 11th note reads;

Burn.

The last paragraph of the 12th note reads;

This collection of notes is itself a note, the 12th note, the final note. It also contains all previous notes and may appear on its own. The 12th note acts as the full sequence of notes on its own. Thus, if you are reading this note, the sequence is complete.
