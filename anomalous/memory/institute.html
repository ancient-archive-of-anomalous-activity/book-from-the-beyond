The Institute

I found two more pages of the book from the beyond. One of them mentioned the existence of an institute that studies things from the beyond. It didn't include any address, or contact information of any kind. At first, I had no idea how to even try to find this place, or what the point of contacting them would even be. But I pondered about it, and realized how lonely and maddening it is to be aware of these things alone. It would be some confirmation that I'm not completely insane, that this obsession isn't for nothing. I tried to find information online with no luck, too many games and stories came up in any search. I started looking around the city for something that looks like it could be the kind of place described in the book. I noticed a few little oddities about the plaques of certain buildings in the city. Little flashes of different text that made it feel like I had misread them at first. I would check their official name, and whatever I had initially “misread” would be the correct name, but blinking once or twice and looking at what's written on the plaque again would reveal, very clearly, a completely different name. This happened with three separate buildings. I'm not sure which one of them could be the institute, the very one mentioned in the book, but I figured I could try to contact them all. I was, disappointingly, too scared to just walk in while.The alternate plaque was visible. Sending regular mail seemed like it would almost definitely get sent to the wrong place and serve as evidence of my lack of cognitive function, so I tried something that, quite frankly, I didn't expect to work. I looked at the email provided for the “correct” institution, and changed the original name for the alternate name in the domain. I did this with all three, and sent them all the same message.

“Hello, apologies for the strangeness of this email, but I am both bad at communicating and at a loss for how to talk about such things. If I have found the right organization this will hopefully make.sense to you, if not, then it is I who has made a mistake and you should ignore this, and I apologize for wasting your time.

I have come into possession of a book with strange qualities that I think are of a similar nature to that of the phenomena that your organization studies, and I think it might be of interest to you. If you would like to know more, please respond to this email, or give me a call.”

And I received the following message back from one of them;

“Dear Mr. Lawrence, we appreciate the effort that contacting us must have taken, however, there are huge security issues regarding discussing anything like this over the phone or through email. Would you be open to discussing this matter with me in person? Sincerely, Adam Bell”

I responded that I would gladly do so, and Dr. Adam Bell and I met at the institute. I showed him the pages I had found, explained their anomalous nature, and added that I had been transcribing whatever I could recall from memory from the rest of the book that I hadn't encountered since early childhood. He seemed unimpressed with what I had assumed were anomalous qualities of the book. He said it could have easily been my imagination in childhood, and the recent appearances of the pages, some sort of prank, or resurfacing of old notes I had written the first time I ‘invented’ the book. But then I pointed out that I only learned of the institute through the book itself, information that he agreed I couldn't have obtained from anywhere else.

He asked if I would be okay with giving him all pages of the book for research, and offered money in return, but I refused, asking if I could give him copies instead. He insisted that I should be the one to get the copied version as the original could have more anomalous traits that should be studied in a lab, and that made sense to me, so I agreed. I asked if he would keep me updated on the findings, out of courtesy, I suppose, and I mentioned how much of my life has been spent obsessing over this book, how important it is to me, which of course, he isn't obligated to care about. But he said it would only be acceptable to keep me updated if I began working for the institute. I asked if they had any job openings and he said he'd send me a list with required qualifications, and to apply to any I qualified for. He also heavily discouraged me from trying to explore the beyond on my own, without the tools and protections the institute could provide. And he told me I could email him again if I find more pieces of the book.

I checked the qualification requirements, and I don't come close to meeting any of them. Knowing me, and my history of academic failure, I highly doubt I'll ever be able to get a job working for them. All I can do is hope they can find me useful in some other way. Now that I've thought about it, I realize how much I hate doing this alone. But I cannot stop doing it altogether. Even with all the risks I've been informed about. I wish people could be given the tools to just safely interact with the beyond, but I must work with what I am given, and these are my only options. Maybe there's at least a way to find others like me. At least I know it's not impossible. At least I can assume that I'm not the only one. I've decided to start publishing my memories of the book and recently rediscovered pages of it in hopes that it might reach someone who's seen similar things. I don't like talking much, personally, but just knowing that I'm not alone would mean everything to me. I just don't know where to try to publish this. What website would be welcoming to such strange encounters? Maybe any of them, it probably doesn't make much of a difference. So, wherever I publish this, I guess this is hello, I'm Martin Laurens. And I'm here to show you the Book From The Beyond.
