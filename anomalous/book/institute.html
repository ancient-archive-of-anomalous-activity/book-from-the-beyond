The Institute

There is an institute founded with the goal of researching and studying things beyond human scientific understanding, and mitigating the danger that these things pose, and the damage that they cause to humans and the world around them. The things that this institute studies are incredibly difficult to research, given that they, in addition to defying natural laws, also cause targeted selective amnesia. It is possible to train oneself to remember, if one persistently practices. This requires one to decide to gaze into the beyond, over and over again, after having forgotten both the beyond, and their resolve to remember it. It is not common for one to make that choice enough times to begin remembering the beasts of the beyond. The institute doesn't face the same challenges when training it's employees to remember, because a senior employee who already rememebrs can remind the people under their mentorship that there is something they need to rememeber, and something they need to see. What they consistently refuse to accept is the fact that this training can only work by changing the nature of those being trained to remember. And the one doing the reminding has already changed long ago. The only way to become capable of coherently perceiving the beasts of the beyond, is to embrace a bit of the beyond within oneself.
