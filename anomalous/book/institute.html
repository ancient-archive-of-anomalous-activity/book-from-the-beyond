The Institute

There is an institute founded with the goal of researching and studying things beyond human scientific understanding, and mitigating the danger that these things pose, and the damage that they cause to humans and the world around them. The things that this institute studies are incredibly difficult to research, given that they, in addition to defying natural laws, also cause targeted selective amnesia. This institute deals with this by training its employees to remember the things these phenomena cause them to forget. What they consistently refuse to accept is the fact that this training can only work by changing the nature of those being trained to remember. The only way to become capable of coherently perceiving the beasts of the beyond, is to embrace a bit of the beyond within themselves.
