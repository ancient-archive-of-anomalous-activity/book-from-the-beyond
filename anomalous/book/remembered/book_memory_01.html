Rewritten Copy Of The Book From The Beyond

Transcribed From Memory By Martin Lawrence

Foreword Of Transcription By Martin Lawrence

There's this strange book that I've known about for as long as I can remember, full of strange drawings and poems about creatures and places that cannot exist, and instructions for elaborate rituals with incomprehensible purposes. For the longest time, I thought it was just a very strange dream that I'd had. I had no idea where I had heard of this book or where I could have possibly seen it. Somehow, I just had the idea that, somewhere out there, it existed. At one point, I had decided to try and find a copy, so, I tried asking people, searching libraries and antique bookshops, and the internet, but there was nothing like it that I could find. It didn't help that I could only remember bits and pieces of it, and the vague sense of doom and urgency that accompanied any memory of the book. I had to forget about it for a while, it had begun to consume my life too much, but I never truly stopped looking for it. And then, it found me. After all these years, the first page was just sitting there, right in front of me, on the sidewalk, waiting for me to find it beneath my feet, on an otherwise uneventful evening walk. It was a miracle. It's only the first page, containing nothing but the foreword, but while it's not much, it still means everything to me. It's proof that the book is actually real, and not just some fever dream I'd had as a kid. It's proof. Tangible, irrefutable proof. Or is it? I suppose it's just a note scrawled in the blue ink of a ballpoint pen, on a piece of lined paper that's been crumpled up and left on the ground, maybe even written by myself. I don't remember. Whatever the case may be, since rediscovering the first page, I've started to remember more and more of the book's contents. Not too much, but, at least a little bit, through fog and dreams. So, I'm writing it down while I still remember. Maybe more will come to me as I write it all down. I want to make my own copy of The Book From The Beyond.
