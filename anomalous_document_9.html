File No. #297-017-2127

Code And ID Number: LCX-2127

Location: Site #297, Formerly Known As Site #19

Protocol: Heavily armed personnel are to guard the perimeter at all times. Triple fences with barbed wire, possibly electric, 6 meters in height, are to be built surrounding the premises. The fences must be strictly well maintained at all times. Never allow them to fall into disrepair. A wall may be built to further secure the area. Researchers are to enter for periods of less than 4 hours at a time. Destroy the location and everything in it. If it is impossible to destroy all the entities and artifacts within the compound, move them to a different site with sufficient security and then begin the destruction of site #19.

Description: Site #19 is a complex of five research and containment buildings that had been used by the institute between the years 1981 and 1992. For reasons that are currently unknown, the facility had been completely wiped from the institute's records and archives, and nobody had any memory of site #19 at all. Staff members initially assigned to work at site #19 left and never returned. They returned to work at different sites instead, and none of them had any memory of having worked at site #19 prior, each having falsely remembered that they had been working at various other locations instead. The exact time and date of when this occurred is uncertain, though based on records found on site, it appears to have completely ceased to keep records of any work or functioning past the date of 17th of May, 1992.
