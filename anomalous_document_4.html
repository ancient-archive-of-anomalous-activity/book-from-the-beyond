Reverse Ritual

The ritual of reversal is one of the harshest rituals that a group of humans can inflict on an individual human. This is because, unlike with most rituals, this ritual is partially carried out by one or multiple entities from the beyond, and the degree to which they can and will torment the sacrifice is completely out of any human's control. The ritual of reversal can be used for two purposes. It can be used to ward off strange malevolent entities from a community, or, it can be used to remove the strange and painful effects that such entities can have on an individual. In the first case, one individual must bear the full effect of the malicious entity, amplified, worsened, proportional to the number of people who will be protected from the malicious entity and length of time they will be protected for. It is very rarely used in the second case, as there are far more effective and gentler rituals that serve the same purpose.

The ritual should be performed in a closed, quiet, completely dark place, with no access to natural light. The larger the space the ritual takes place in, the more intense the ritual will be, intense here meaning both more painful, and more effective. The sacrifice should be laying down flat on their back. A straight line of light will appear somewhere over their body, the exact length and location may vary. The light will steadily increase in brightness and heat for a short time, until the sacrifice is completely enveloped by the light and can no longer be seen by anyone around them. The sacrifice may be audible to the people in the room with them, though they themselves will not be able to hear anyone or anything during the remainder of the ritual.

It is at this point that the sacrifice may or may not begin to scream. The screaming must not be interrupted or soothed, no matter how long it lasts. The screaming occurs because something from the beyond is performing their side of the ritual, and interruption is regarded as a refusal to cooperate, a breaking of the oath. Sedatives and tranquilizers and painkillers and alcohol will not be effective. There is nothing you can do for the sacrifice past this point. Saving them now will anger the beyond more than never having offered anyone in the first place. You can't stop the suffering once it's begun. You can only make it worse. If you terminate the ritual, the entities from the beyond will retaliate. Do not start the ritual unless you are certain that you have it in yourself to be cruel enough to let it continue. There will be devastating consequences if it is performed disrespectfully or not carried out fully.

After some time, the light will begin to fade away, and the sacrifice will slowly regain their ability to hear their peers. The ritual is complete once the light is completely gone. The sacrifice will usually be left with a scar where the light had shone at the start of the ritual, usually a burn, though sometimes it looks like the slice of a blade. The ritual may last anywhere between a few minutes to several hours. After the ritual is over, a strange black substance may be found on the floor. Simply clean the floor as usual. Do not let the liquid sit uncleaned for too long.
