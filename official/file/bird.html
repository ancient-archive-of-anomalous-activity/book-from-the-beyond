File No. #477-014-1124

Code And ID#: ECX-1124

Location: Site #477, Formerly Known As Site #119

Protocol: A team of at least 3 may enter Site #477 for less than 4 hours at a time. Document and record every instance of a bird found to be doing anything, especially if it is behaving violently, canibalistically, or is found to be injured or dead. If they become violent or agressive towards any human for any reason, immediatly retreat and shoot as necessarry. Both dead and living birds may be retrieved for study whenever possible. These expeditions are to be done approximatley once a week.

Description: Entity #1124 is a multitude of birds found to appear within a 5 kilometer radius of site #477 with seemingly no origin from the surrounding ecosystem. The number of birds appearing and dying within site #477 sould be noticed in statistics related to local bird populations, but it has been found that this is not the case, and local bird poulations are never effected by the birds that appear within site #477. No birds taken out of site #477 survive for longer than 24 hours. Birds that are found to be injured or dying within site #477 never recover, even if their injuries would be survivable if present in a non-anomalous off-site bird, even with high quality vet care, though their lifespan is longer if not removed from site #477. Many birds become hostile at random if observed by humans for too long, with significantly increased rates of violent behaviour if observed for longer than 4 hours at a time.

Prior to incident #IR-477-014-07-12-23, these birds had been found in an abandoned shed in the nearby woodland, and were confined to a 5 kilometer radius of it. Their behaviour has not changed. They were originally documented by the now decommissioned site #119. It is, as of yet, still unknown how, or why the epicenter of this phenomenon changed after incident #IR-477-014-07-12-23.
