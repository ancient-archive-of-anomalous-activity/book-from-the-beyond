Location: UNCONTAINED

Protocol: Censor anything discovered to be a piece of the Book that the public broadly has access to, and cannot be contained. Only attempt destruction if unanimously requested by the high council. Due to the broad and scattered nature of the Book, it is impossible to fully contain it. However, it is very much possible to contain excerpts and pieces of it. Many have been already documented within their own separate files at various different sites of the facility. Contain pieces of the book separatley from standard files and documents. Sections of the archives containing pieced of the Book From The Beyond should have the highest security standards applied to them. Speciffic containment measures may be determined on a case by case basis.

Description: "The Book From The Beyond" is a series of written, drawn, and otherwise recorded information about what is reffered to as the 'Beasts of the Beyond'. The book appears to have no author, it's drawings have no artist, over all, it has no creator. Pieces of the book simply apppear into existence without the process of creation ever taking place. There have been instances of writings and drawings confirmed to have been created by human beings, presumbably using normal methods of creation, being included in the Book Of The Beyond, alongside the authorless writings and drawings that compose the vast majority of the book. It is often difficult to recognize a piece of the Book From The Beyond, as they tend to hide in plain sight, but they always have some sort of anomalous properties that give them away if observed long enough.

Everything described in the Book is anomalous in nature. When compared to documented anomalous phenomena in the instittue's archives, the majority of the book's described phenomena can be confimred to be real, and the majority of the descriptions have been confirmed to be, for the most part, accurate. Entries in the book have been used to find further phenomena to study and contain in many cases.

It is unknown how long the Book has existed for, or if there is any anomalous phenomenon not recorded by it. The earliest confirmed entry has been found to be a series of cave paintings dating back to around 60,000 years ago. Some of the most recent confirmed entries have been found online in the form of videos, images, and social media posts. The mediums that the "Book From The Beyond" is composed of also include but are not limited to; seemingly handwirtten notes, typed and printed documents, oil paintings, ancient statues, engravings of various kinds, VHS tapes, film reels, CDs, MP3 files, MP4 files, and many, many more. Any method of communication may be used as a way to convey bits and pieces of the Book From The Beyond.

The Book appears to contain several descriptions of the institute, likely due to it's proximity to the paranomal, given that this is what we research here. The theory that some have proposed, that the institute is spoken of by the book because the institute itself is, somehow, anomalous, can be assumed to be entirely false.
