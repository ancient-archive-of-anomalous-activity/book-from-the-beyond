File No. #375-018-2898

Code And ID#: ORX-2898

Location: Site #375

WARNING: To be used in cases of extreme anomalous impairment, injury or disfigurment. Not for use in treatment of mild anomalous amnesia.

Instructions: Administer sedatives or painkillers prior to the procedure on a case by case basis, as determined by a physician on staff. The procedure should not hurt.

WARNING: UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES SHOULD THE PROCEDURE EVER BE INTERRUPTED. During the procedure, the patient may speak, though they will remain unable to hear anyone until the procedure is complete.

Place patient in chamber lying flat on their back. The machine will make an incision acrross the body. The incision will heal within a few hours after the procedure. Hold the patient for monitoring for up to 24 hours. No special post operational care is required. The patient may immediatly return to theri regular activities upon being released home.
