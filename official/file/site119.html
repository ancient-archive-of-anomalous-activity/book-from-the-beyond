File No. #297-014-2790

Code And ID#: LCX-2790

Location: Site #477, Formerly Known As Site #119

Protocol: Heavily armed personnel are to guard the perimeter at all times. Triple fences with barbed wire, possibly electric, 6 meters in height, are to be built surrounding the premises. The fences must be strictly well maintained at all times. Never allow them to fall into disrepair. A wall may be built to further secure the area. Researchers are to enter in groups of at least 3 for less than 4 hours at a time. Destroy the location and everything in it. If it is impossible to destroy all the entities and artifacts within the compound, move them to a different site with sufficient security and then begin the destruction of site #119.

Description: Site #477 is a complex of five research and containment buildings that had been used by the institute between the years 1981 and 1992. For reasons that are currently unknown, the facility had been completely wiped from the institute's records and archives, and no staff members from any of the instittues active branches had had any memory of site #119 existing at all. Staff members that had been assigned to work at site #119 had left and never returned. They had returned to work at different sites instead, and none of them had any memory of having worked at site #119, each having falsely remembered that they had been working at various other locations instead. The exact time and date of when site #119 became compromised is uncertain, though based on records found at site #119 itself, it appears to have completely ceased keeping records of any activity occuring on site past the date of 17th of May, 1992.

Venturing into site #119 alone causes spontaneous lacerations, sudden organ failure, dramatically increased risk of heart attacks and seizures in otherwise healthy individuals, hypertension, hypothermia, sudden starvaion syndrome, severe burns, headaches dizziness, nausea, vommiting and death. Entering the premisis with another person halves these risks. With three or more people, the risk of serious injury or health consequences becomes negligable. However, no matter the group size, staying on the premises for over four hours elevates the risk to the same degree as entering it completely alone in the first place. AS long as three or more individuals are present at the location for less than four hours at a time, the only anomalous effect the site has is causing an inability to coherently perceive one's surroundings. This may present as hallucinations or the inability to see something that actually is there. Often people will collectively hallucinate the exact same thing. Sometimes a group will have several collective hallucinations. While this makes proper documentation of incidents very difficult, at the very least, it seems to have no lasting consequences on the people sent to investigate the location.

Most of the objects and entities that were contained at site #119 have left the site, and those remaining have since been recontained at other sites, thus leaving site #119 completely stripped of any anomalous creatures or object that could still be affecting it. Despite this, site #119 seems to have retained some of it's anomalous qualities and must now itself be contained and documented as yet another anomalous location. Site #119 is thus being relabeled to location #2790, and the structure surrounding it, meant to safely isolate it, has been labeled as a new site, namely, site #477.

Only a five objects and two entities that had been contained at site #119 had remained there until the arrival of the cleanup crew. All of them, with the exceptions of object #2791 and entity #1124, both of which have since been relocated and redocumented according to protocol. Most objects and entites that had been contained at site #119 have been lost. Some were still documented in the archives, but many were wiped off the record altogether. Of those wiped off the record, some have been remembered by a few former employees and have been redocumented based on memory, though these files should be updated once the phenomena they describe are found again. Some that were completely forgotten have left behind enough evidence to be recodumented based on said evidence alone. There is some evidence that is inconclusive, but indicates the existence of some completely forgotten entities that will have to be rediscovered. Some have been recaptured and redocumented.
