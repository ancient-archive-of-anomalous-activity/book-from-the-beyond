Foreword For A Transcribed Copy Of The Book From The Beyond

The Beasts from the Beyond bleed. And their blood becomes words and drawings. Humans from here will see the words that are written, and the drawings that are drawn. And they will ask who wrote them and who drew them. But nobody wrote them and nobody drew them. The Book From The Beyond does not need an author, it simply is. Reading The Book From The Beyond has consequences, the same consequences as touching anything else from the Beyond, it becomes a part of you, in confusing and complicated ways. The choice to keep reading this is yours and yours alone. The next piece of the Book will find you if you look for it.

Those were the words written on a piece of paper I found on the sidewalk. And I do believe them. Because this wasn't my first encounter with this object.

There's this strange book that I had known about for as long as I could remember, full of strange drawings and poems about creatures and places that cannot exist, and instructions for elaborate rituals with incomprehensible purposes. For the longest time, I thought it was just a very strange dream that I'd had. I had no idea where I had heard of this book or where I'd seen it. Somehow I just had the idea that somewhere out there, it existed. At one point I decided to try and find a copy, so I tried asking people, librarians, and the internet, but there was nothing like it that I could find. It didn't help that I could only remember bits and pieces of it, nothing too concrete. I've been trying to find it my entire life. And then, it found me. After all those years, that note was waiting for me to find it beneath my feet. It was a miracle. Or a curse. But at least I've found what seems to be the first page. And it reminded me, at least a little bit, of the exact contents of the book. So, I'm writing it down while I still remember. I want to make my own copy of The Book From The Beyond.

The ritual of reversal can be used for two purposes. It can be used to ward off strange malevolent entities from a community, or, it can be used to remove the strange and painful effects that such entities can have on an individual.

In the first case, one individual must bear the full effect of the malicious entity, amplified, worsened, to account for the number of people and length of time they will be protected for. It is one of the harshest rituals that a group of humans can inflict on someone, mostly because, unlike with most rituals, the malicious entity participates in the ritual, and the degree to which it can and will torment the sacrifice is completely out of anyone's control.

It is very rarely used in the second case, as there are far more effective and gentler rituals that serve the same purpose.

The ritual requires the sacrifice to be placed in a closed space of some kind, the larger the space, the more intense the ritual will be, which may be desirable for larger groups, longer protection, or stronger entities.

I don't remember the steps to the ritual anymore. It's been years since I've read them. But I do remember some of what happens once the ritual begins.

The sacrifice should be laying down flat on their back. A line of light will appear over their body, starting somewhere near the shoulders on the torso, and ending near the navel on the abdomen. The light will steadily increase in brightness and heat for a few minutes, until the sacrifice is completely enveloped by the light and impossible to see by anyone around them, but it will not extend further beyond the body of the sacrifice, and the dark room the ritual is occurring in will remain dark. The light will continue keeping the sacrifice within itself for some time, during which the sacrifice may be audible to the persons in the room with them, though they will not be able to hear anyone in the room should they try to speak to them. After some time, the light will begin to fade away, and the sacrifice will slowly regain their ability to hear their peers. The ritual is complete once the light is completely gone. The sacrifice will usually be left with a scar where the light had shone at the start of the ritual, usually deeper at the end points, usually a burn, though sometimes it looks more like it's been sliced into with a blade of some sort. The ritual may last anywhere between a few minutes to several hours. During the ritual, the sacrifice might start to scream. The screaming must not be interrupted or soothed, no matter how long it lasts.

Something from the beyond is performing their side of the ritual, and interruption is regarded as a refusal to cooperate, a breaking of the oath. Sedatives and tranquilizers and painkillers and alcohol will not be effective. There is nothing you can do for the sacrifice past this point. Saving them now will anger the beyond more than never having offered anyone in the first place. You can't stop the suffering once it's begun. You can only make it worse.

I don't remember how bad it can get, but I do remember the book going into detail about it. I remember there were many rituals involving many kinds of torture, but none allowed for the potential for things to get this bad. This is described by the book as the worst ritual in existence, though I'd argue such a label must be subjective, and if the book truly doesn't have an author, then where is that subjectivity coming from? I don't know. I don't remember anymore. I need to find the rest of the book and read as much of it as I can. I know it has consequences. I know it will eat me alive. But it is an impulse that hurts to deny. If you stumble upon it, you've been warned, make whatever choice you see fit.
