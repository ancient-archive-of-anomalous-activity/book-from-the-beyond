AUDIO LOG TRANSCRIPT: DR. ADAM BELL AND MARTIN LAWRENCE

MARTIN: Please, Dr. Bell, please, I can't live like this anymore, I can't keep letting it swallow more and more of my life...

DR. BELL: It's incredibly unsta-

MARTIN: According to every scrap of documentation I could find, the first experiment went exactly as planned! It was a resounding success! Anything that's going to be used on humans needs to be tested on people like-

DR. BELL: The side effects are massive! We developed these machines for severe cases that couldn't be helped by any other means, no matter what we tried! It was always meant to be used extremely rarely for exceptionally difficult cases!

MARTIN: Konrad Kovač's case wasn't much worse than my own!

DR. BELL: Exactly, and that's the tragedy of it! Mr. Kovač proved to us that it wasn't worth using in cases like his and yours! Go visit him. Ask him if he has any regrets. He's living in a mental institution now, think about that.

MARTIN: If I don't get any help with this I'm guaranteed to end up in a mental institution anyway!

DR. BELL: Mr. Lawrence, stop. Listen to me. I am about to tell you this because, for everyone's sake, I need you to stop begging me to put you in that chamber. What I am about to tell you is highly classified. You mustn't tell anyone ever, not even a hint, not even in your sleep, not to a lover, not to a therapist, not even a lawyer, nobody, understood?

DR. BELL: Good.

DR. BELL: The machine was never built to cure anything. It isn't actually a medical device. There will be no more testing because it was never intended to go through clinical trials as a true medical procedure. It's not actually a cure for anything. It didn't heal Mr. Kovač of anything at all. It did horribly damage him. The experiment was a resounding success, not because it healed Konrad, but because it hurt him more. Because it isn't a medical device. And we weren't testing its ability to heal. We cannot put you in the device with the intent to cure you because it wasn't designed to cure anyone. It is not a medical device. The documentation is all fake. So, for the love of sanity, stop asking to be put into the bloody torture chamber!

MARTIN: It's- it's not a medical device?

DR. BELL: No.

MARTIN: Well then what the hell is it?

DR. BELL: You really don't need to know that.

MARTIN: Please, I deserve at least that much. You know better than anybody how these things tend to eat away at me.

DR. BELL: I will tell you, and then you will stop asking me about it, is that clear?

MARTIN: Yes.

DR. BELL: It was built to capture and kill the demon Razizrak. We are to make an attempt as soon as next week. After that, the machine might be used on a few more entities. But it's more likely that we'll just dismantle it once Razizrak is neutralized.

MARTIN: Razizrak? Is he really that big of a threat that you'd build a machine, and torture a man to insanity to test it, just for him and him alone?

DR. BELL: You know of Razizrak?

MARTIN: I vaguely remember reading about him, yes.

DR. BELL: You probably don't have the full picture, then. All you have is half coherent memories from an unconfirmed source.

MARTIN: I suppose so.

DR. BELL: Do you understand now? Will you drop it?

MARTIN: Sure, fine, I'm sorry. I didn't know.

DR. BELL: You weren't supposed to know. Make sure I won't regret telling you.

MARTIN: You won't, I swear.

DR. BELL: Good. We're done here. Goodbye, Mr. Lawrence.
